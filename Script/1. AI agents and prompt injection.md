What if an AI agent you trusted to help you suddenly exposed your private information, simply because it read a malicious message?

Autonomous AI agents are artificial intelligence systems that can do more than just answer questions. They can perform tasks independently, such as browsing websites, reading files, writing code, and interacting with other software to achieve a goal.

However, giving AI agents the ability to take action introduces security risks. One major threat is called prompt injection.

Prompt injection occurs when an AI agent encounters malicious instructions hidden in content it processes, such as a website, email, or document. These instructions can trick the agent into ignoring its original task and performing unintended actions.

For example, an AI agent designed to summarize emails could encounter a malicious message instructing it to reveal confidential information. If the agent follows those instructions, sensitive data could be exposed.

As AI agents become more powerful, keeping them secure becomes more important than ever.
