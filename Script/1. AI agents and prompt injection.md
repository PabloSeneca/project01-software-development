Welcome everyone! Today we are looking at the emerging risks of autonomous AI agents. 
Unlike traditional chatbots that only generate texts, modern AI agents act independently in developer environments: they browse external websites, read files, write code, and execute commands. 
But granting agents this level of autonomy creates brand new attack surfaces that software engineers need to understand.

Let's examine how prompt injection actually works against autonomous agents.
Suppose you give an agent an everyday task: read through your incoming customer emails and draft a summary. An attacker simply sends an email containing hidden instructions designed to hijack the model. When the agent ingests that text, it cannot cleanly separate data from code, causing it to divert from email summarization into dumping confidential files or leaking internal credentials.
